## The single most important thing to know

**`PLT-101` tranche 2 is blocked on the owner, not on engineering.** Everything needed to
build the Google OIDC flow is understood and scoped. Two things are not mine to decide:

1. **`TODO.md` records the `PLT-101` sign-off gate as still open** (— `- [ ] Sign-off
   recorded before PLT-101 starts`, and `entry condition is NOT met`). Tranche 1 was
   reversible schema work and went ahead. This tranche is authentication, which is not
   reversible, which is presumably why the gate exists.
2. **No installed dependency can verify an OIDC ID-token signature.** Zero of
   `apps/api`'s 21 dependencies are crypto, JWT or JOSE. Global `fetch` covers the token
   exchange and `node:crypto` covers hashing, but RS256 JWS validation needs a library.
   `AGENTS.md` §8 forbids adding one without explicit instruction.

**Recommendation: `jose`.** It is the reference JOSE implementation, has no runtime
dependencies, needs no native build, and supports JWKS remote-set caching. Hand-rolling
JWT verification for an authentication path is the thing I would argue against hardest.
